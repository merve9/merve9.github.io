Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

"MLP Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Mia, Leo & Pumpkin to upload and manage videos on that channel.

What data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload videos, set video titles, descriptions, playlists and publishing times, and read aggregated channel performance reports.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect any personal information from viewers, including children.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the purposes above. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Mia, Leo & Pumpkin YouTube channel.
